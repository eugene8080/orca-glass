Read your Orca + Hermes sessions. Speak your next step.

Orca Glass connects G2 glasses to agents running in Orca on your Windows PC, and to Hermes Agent. Read conversations, see working/waiting status and the model, and review spoken replies before sending.

Version 1.0.36:
- Filter by agent: Hermes Agent, or All Orca Sessions / Claude / Codex / other Orca agents. Hermes and Orca sessions stay separate.
- Filter by workspace: Orca shows recent workspaces first, then the rest; Hermes shows Bots, Sessions and Scheduled jobs.
- Conversations open at the start of the latest reply. Nine rows per page; swipe for pages, swipe down on the last page to refresh. One status row shows state, model and page, e.g. /Waiting Opus5.5 Pg7/8.
- Hold to open a full-screen voice page that is already listening; release to review, click to send, double-click to cancel. Whisper runs locally on your PC and ignores silence.
- For numbered prompts, click for choices or speak an option number, then confirm.
- View Codex, Claude and Cursor usage; toggle resets between computer time, phone time and time remaining.
- Start Codex in an existing Orca workspace.

Requires G2, the Even Realities Android app, Orca and the companion Windows bridge. The packaged connection targets a configured private PC over Tailscale; arbitrary-PC setup is not yet self-service. Agents run on your PC, not the glasses. English only. Try the demo without a PC.
