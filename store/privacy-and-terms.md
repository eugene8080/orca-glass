# Orca Glass
## Privacy Policy
Updated 3 November 2026 | Developer: Quiet Signal

### No developer collection of app data
Orca Glass connects your glasses and phone to a companion bridge on a PC you control. Quiet Signal does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your microphone recordings, transcripts, conversations, prompts, session details or account-usage data through the app. The app has no Quiet Signal analytics, advertising or tracking service. Processing on your devices is not collection by Quiet Signal.

### How your data is processed
When you start voice input, microphone audio travels from the glasses through the Even Realities app to your PC bridge. Whisper transcribes it locally on that PC. You review the transcript before sending it to the selected desktop agent; cancelling does not send it to that agent. Conversation text, session titles, activity and available usage information travel from your PC to the app for display. The bridge uses your local Orca sessions and permissions.

Microphone access enables voice input. Network access connects to the PC bridge. Orca Glass does not request location, push-notification or direct Bluetooth access, and does not track your location. The Even Realities app manages the glasses connection under its own permissions and privacy policy.

### Your devices, accounts and service providers
Your Tailscale connection and any LLM services are configured and controlled under your own accounts. A confirmed instruction may be processed by the LLM provider used by your selected desktop agent. This is your use of that service, not Quiet Signal collecting your content and sharing it with a provider. Those services and Even Realities have their own policies, retention settings and terms.

The development/prototype connection mode can use a Cloudflare tunnel and ntfy discovery instead of a fixed bridge origin. In that mode, Cloudflare carries bridge traffic and ntfy receives signed connection-discovery metadata. Use only a connection you control and understand. Quiet Signal does not operate a hosted bridge or receive this traffic as the app developer.

### Storage and your controls
Pairing credentials and display preferences are stored through the Even app's local storage. Audio is buffered for transcription; the bridge does not intentionally save recordings or log microphone transcripts. Orca, your agents and your PC may retain conversations and diagnostic logs according to their own configuration. Your providers may retain data under their policies. Quiet Signal has no central copy of this app data to retrieve or erase.

You can stop processing by closing the app or stopping the bridge, revoke microphone access, and manage pairing credentials, local histories and provider records through their respective controls. Uninstalling Orca Glass does not necessarily delete PC histories or records held by your providers.

### Support and privacy enquiries
If you choose to email quietsignal.apps@gmail.com, Quiet Signal receives your email address and whatever you include, through Gmail. This voluntary support correspondence is separate from app operation. We use it to answer your request and keep necessary support records, not for marketing. Do not send credentials or private conversations. You can request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to applicable retention requirements. We publish an updated policy when these practices change.

## Terms of Use
Updated 3 November 2026 | Developer: Quiet Signal

### Scope and requirements
Orca Glass is an independent Windows/Android fork of Orca Glass by 2ero20ne. It is a companion interface for coding agents running on your PC, not an LLM hosting service. You supply compatible glasses, the Even Realities app, Orca, a configured PC bridge, network connectivity and any required provider accounts. Some test builds are restricted to a configured PC and are not a self-service installation for arbitrary PCs.

### Your control and responsibilities
Use only devices, workspaces, accounts and content you are authorised to access. Protect your pairing credentials and review the selected session and transcript before sending. Instructions sent to an agent may cause actions on your PC or through your accounts. Your agent's permissions and approval settings govern those actions. You remain responsible for configuring them and for complying with the terms of services you use.

Speech recognition and AI output can be wrong. Review output and proposed actions before relying on them. Features and compatibility may change with updates to the glasses, Even SDK, Orca or provider tools. No uninterrupted availability or support response time is promised.
